Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours and typically arrive in 2–4 working days in major cities and 4–6 days elsewhere.

What is your exchange policy?

Unworn items with tags can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery for a different size or product. Fragrances are exchangeable only if sealed.

How do I find my size?

Open any product and tap Size guide for chest, waist and length measurements. Between sizes? We recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes — cash on delivery is available nationwide.
